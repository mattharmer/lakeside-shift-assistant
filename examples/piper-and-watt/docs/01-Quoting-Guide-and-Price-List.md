# Quoting Guide and Price List

**Piper & Watt Plumbing & Electrical**  
Newcastle and Lake Macquarie, NSW  
Document 01 | Version 1.0 | November 2026  
Internal use only

## 1. Purpose

Use this guide when a customer asks for a price, when you write a quote, or when you explain a job card total.

Every figure in this document is an **example price in Australian dollars, inclusive of GST**, set for this company as at November 2026. Confirm the current schedule in the office before you give a figure to a customer. These are not a public offer, and they are not a survey of other local rates.

## 2. Who may give a price

- **Office / Scheduler (ext. 1):** may give a firm schedule price for items listed as firm in this document, during standard hours, with the assumptions below.
- **Licensed Plumbing Supervisor (ext. 2):** reviews plumbing and drainage quotes, all hot water replacements, and any quote over $1,000.
- **Licensed Electrical Supervisor (ext. 3):** reviews all electrical quotes, all switchboard work, and any work that needs a compliance certificate.
- **Tradespeople and apprentices on site:** may explain a schedule item already agreed, and may take a variation to the supervisor. Do not invent a new price on the driveway.

If you are unsure, say so, take a name and number, and call back. Do not guess.

## 3. Standard hours and after hours

- **Standard hours:** Monday to Friday, 7:00 am to 4:30 pm, and Saturday 7:00 am to 12:00 noon for booked, non-emergency work only.
- **After hours:** from close until 7:00 am, all day Sunday, and public holidays. After-hours attendance is for emergencies, or when the office has booked it and told the customer the after-hours rate.

Schedule lump-sum prices in sections 6 and 7 are for **standard hours only**. Do not use them after hours. After hours, use the after-hours call-out and after-hours labour in section 5.

## 4. How to quote

1. Confirm the job is in our usual service area (Newcastle, Lake Macquarie, and nearby Hunter suburbs as booked by the office) and is work we do.
2. Confirm whether the visit is standard hours or after hours.
3. Use a firm schedule price only when the job matches the description and assumptions in this document.
4. For any item marked **from**, and for work that is not listed as a firm price, book a site inspection. Do not give a firm figure.
5. Write the quote on the quoting sheet or in the job app: what is included, what is not, the example or quoted total in AUD incl. GST, and that the quote is valid for 14 days.
6. Quotes over $1,000, and any switchboard upgrade, hot water replacement, or work that needs a compliance certificate, must be reviewed by the relevant licensed supervisor before they go to the customer.
7. Parts not held on the van are quoted and approved by the customer before you order them. Extra travel to collect parts is billable at the labour rate only if the office has approved it.
8. Payment, deposits, cancellation, and warranty are in Document 03.

## 9. What to say on the phone

1. Give a firm schedule price only when the job matches a firm item above.
2. Say the price is in dollars inclusive of GST, and that it assumes the conditions in this guide.
3. For "from" items, say: "I can give you a starting figure of [the from price], but we need a site inspection and a written quote from the licensed supervisor before that is firm."
4. If the customer wants a price we cannot give, book the inspection and take a callback. Do not invent a middle figure to keep them on the line.
