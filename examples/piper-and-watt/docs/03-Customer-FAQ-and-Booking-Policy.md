# Customer FAQ and Booking Policy

**Piper & Watt Plumbing & Electrical**  
Newcastle and Lake Macquarie, NSW  
Document 03 | Version 1.0 | November 2026  
Internal use only

## 1. Purpose

Use this document when you book a job, take an after-hours call, or answer a customer question about warranty, payment, or cancellation.

Give only what this document and Document 01 say. If you are unsure, take a callback and ask the office (ext. 1) or the relevant licensed supervisor.

## 2. Who we are and where we work

Piper & Watt Plumbing & Electrical is a plumbing, drainage, and electrical company serving homes and small businesses in Newcastle, Lake Macquarie, and nearby Hunter suburbs as booked by the office.

Jobs outside the usual area need office approval before you promise a visit.

We do licensed plumbing, drainage, and electrical work. We do not take on work we are not licensed for. Electrical work is carried out only by a licensed electrician, or by an apprentice under the direct supervision of a licensed electrician. Do not tell a customer they can do electrical work themselves.

The office phone number and email are on the invoice, the vans, and the yard noticeboard. Do not invent a number.

## 3. Hours, booking and after-hours

- **Standard hours:** Monday to Friday, 7:00 am to 4:30 pm.
- **Saturday:** 7:00 am to 12:00 noon, booked non-emergency work only.
- **Sunday and public holidays:** emergency call-outs only.
- **After hours:** from close until 7:00 am, plus Sunday and public holidays, through the on-call licensed tradesperson. The on-call roster is on the office board. Do not publish a personal mobile number.

Book standard work through the office (ext. 1) in the job app. Take the customer's name, mobile, address, access notes, a short description of the fault, and whether anyone over 18 will be on site. Ask for a photo if it will help the supervisor decide the trade and the time needed.

When you have booked the job, give the customer the arrival window on the job card, not a promise of an exact minute.

## 4. What counts as an emergency

Treat these as after-hours emergencies and offer the on-call attendance, using the after-hours rates in Document 01:

- A burst or badly leaking pipe that cannot be isolated, or water causing damage.
- No hot water in the home, where the customer cannot reasonably wait until standard hours.
- Sewage overflow or a completely blocked toilet in a home with no other working toilet.
- A complete loss of power in the home.
- Sparking, burning smell, or a fitting that is hot or discoloured at the electrical installation.

If there is immediate danger — fire, heavy smoke, a person injured, or a live electrical hazard — tell the customer to call **000** first, get people away, and wait for emergency services. Then take the job details and call the on-call supervisor.

**Emergency wording you may give a customer (and no more):**

- If it is safe to do so, they may switch the home off at the main switch on the meter-box door. They should not open the switchboard, not touch exposed wiring, and not try to test or repair anything.
- If they smell burning, see sparks, or cannot safely reach the main switch, they should leave the area and call 000.
- For a burst pipe, they may shut the water at the meter if they can do so safely, and should not enter floodwater if electrical fittings may be wet.

Do not add do-it-yourself repair steps, wiring steps, or isolation steps beyond that wording.

## 5. Payment terms

- Domestic customers: payment is due on completion, by card or EFT, unless the office has approved an account.
- Approved account customers: 14 days from the invoice date. Only the office can approve an account.
- Supply-and-install quotes of $1,000 or more: a 30% deposit is due when the customer accepts the quote, unless the written quote says otherwise. The balance is due on completion.
- All prices and invoices are in AUD inclusive of GST.
- We do not offer informal "mates' rates" or cash-only jobs. If a customer asks, decline and follow this policy.

If a customer disputes an invoice, do not argue on the driveway. Take the details, stay courteous, and refer it to the office and the relevant licensed supervisor.

## 6. Quotes and cancellation

- Written quotes are valid for 14 days from the quote date, unless the quote says a shorter period for a priced part.
- For cancellation of **booked standard-hours work:** more than 24 hours' notice, no charge. Less than 24 hours' notice, or no one home when we arrive, and the standard call-out of $165 may be charged.
- For **after-hours or emergency work:** once the tradesperson has been dispatched, the after-hours call-out of $295 may be charged if the customer cancels or is not on site.
- If we have already ordered a special part with the customer's approval, the cost of that part may also be charged if it cannot be returned.

Tell the customer this when you book a job or send a quote, and point them to the terms on the quote or invoice.

## 7. Warranty

- Our labour is warranted for 12 months from the invoice date.
- Parts and equipment we supply carry the manufacturer's warranty, as noted on the invoice.
- Warranty does not cover misuse, work carried out by others, lack of maintenance, or faults outside the original scope.
- To make a claim, the customer should call the office with the invoice number. We will inspect the work before we agree a remedy.
- Do not promise a longer warranty, a cash refund, or a full replacement unless the licensed supervisor has approved it.

## 8. Access, safety and what we need on site

- Someone 18 or over should be on site, or the customer must have arranged access in advance with the office.
- Please secure dogs and keep children clear of the work area, ladders, pits, and the van.
- We need clear access to the meter box, switchboard, hot water system, or the fitting we are there to work on.
- If we arrive and the site is unsafe, we will stop, explain why, and contact the supervisor. We will not start against the pre-start in Document 02.

## 9. Complaints

Take the customer's name, contact details, invoice or job number, and a short description of the concern. Thank them for telling us, do not admit fault on the spot, and tell them the office or the relevant licensed supervisor will call back.

Refer complaints to the Office / Scheduler (ext. 1). The Licensed Plumbing Supervisor (ext. 2) or Licensed Electrical Supervisor (ext. 3) will take over if the complaint is about workmanship or safety.

## 10. Short answers for common questions

- **"How much will it be?"** Use Document 01. Give a firm schedule price only when the job matches a firm item. Otherwise book a site inspection.
- **"Can you come tonight?"** If it is an emergency as in section 4, yes, through the on-call tradesperson, at after-hours rates. If it can wait, offer the next standard-hours window.
- **"Can you tell me how to wire it / fix the board myself?"** No. In NSW, electrical work is carried out by a licensed electrician. Offer to book us, and if there is danger, tell them to call 000. Do not give steps.
- **"Can I pay next month?"** Only if they already have an approved account. Otherwise payment is due on completion. Refer special requests to the office.
- **"Will you beat another quote?"** We quote from this company's schedule and a site inspection. Do not undercut a figure you have not had approved by the supervisor.
