# Cocktail Spec Sheet

**The Lakeside Hotel**  
Lake Macquarie, NSW  
Document 03 | Version 1.0 | November 2026  
Internal use only

## 1. House rules

1. Use this sheet for the ten house classics. Measure every pour with a jigger. Do not free-pour cocktails.
2. Use fresh citrus and 1:1 sugar syrup unless a recipe says otherwise.
3. Follow the glass, ice, method, and garnish listed. If a guest wants a change, say so on the ticket and check with the Bar Supervisor if you are unsure.
4. Ask before using egg white. Tell the guest it contains egg. Do not serve egg white if they have an egg allergy, or if they do not want it.
5. RSA still applies. Do not make or serve a cocktail for a person you believe is intoxicated, or for a guest who cannot produce acceptable ID when asked. See Document 02.
6. If a product is 86'd, tell the Duty Manager or Bar Supervisor and offer a listed alternative only if you know the spec.

All volumes below are house specs in millilitres (ml).

## 2. Margarita

- **Glass:** Coupe (straight up) or rocks (on ice). Ask the guest.
- **Ice:** Cubed ice for shaking. Use fresh ice in the glass if serving on the rocks.
- **Method:**
  1. Salt a half rim if the guest asks for salt. Leave the rim plain if they do not.
  2. Shake tequila, triple sec, and lime juice hard with ice.
  3. Fine-strain into the glass (over fresh ice if on the rocks).
- **Specs:** 50 ml blanco tequila · 25 ml triple sec · 25 ml fresh lime juice · 10 ml sugar syrup only if the lime is very sharp
- **Garnish:** Lime wheel or lime wedge.

## 3. Espresso Martini

- **Glass:** Coupe.
- **Ice:** Cubed ice for shaking only. Serve straight up.
- **Method:**
  1. Pull a fresh short espresso and let it sit for a moment so it is hot but not surging.
  2. Shake vodka, coffee liqueur, espresso, and sugar syrup very hard with ice.
  3. Fine-strain into the glass. The top should have a thick crema.
- **Specs:** 50 ml vodka · 25 ml coffee liqueur · 30 ml fresh espresso · 10 ml sugar syrup
- **Garnish:** Three coffee beans on the crema.

## 4. Mojito

- **Glass:** Highball.
- **Ice:** Crushed ice.
- **Method:**
  1. Place mint, lime juice, and sugar syrup in the glass. Press gently. Do not shred the mint.
  2. Add the rum. Fill the glass with crushed ice and churn.
  3. Top with soda. Add a little more crushed ice.
- **Specs:** 50 ml white rum · 25 ml fresh lime juice · 15 ml sugar syrup · 8 to 10 mint leaves · soda water, to top
- **Garnish:** Mint sprig and a lime wedge.

## 5. Negroni

- **Glass:** Rocks.
- **Ice:** One large cube, or fresh cubed ice.
- **Method:**
  1. Stir gin, Campari, and sweet vermouth with ice until cold.
  2. Strain over fresh ice in the rocks glass.
- **Specs:** 30 ml gin · 30 ml Campari · 30 ml sweet vermouth
- **Garnish:** Orange peel. Express the oils over the drink and drop the peel in.

## 6. Old Fashioned

- **Glass:** Rocks.
- **Ice:** One large cube.
- **Method:**
  1. Stir whisky, sugar syrup, and bitters with ice until cold and slightly diluted.
  2. Strain over a large cube.
  3. If using a sugar cube instead of syrup, muddle the cube with the bitters and a dash of water first, then add whisky and stir.
- **Specs:** 60 ml bourbon or rye (ask if the guest has a preference) · 10 ml sugar syrup or 1 sugar cube · 2 dashes Angostura bitters
- **Garnish:** Orange peel, oils expressed over the drink. A cherry is optional.

## 7. Aperol Spritz

- **Glass:** Wine glass.
- **Ice:** Cubed ice, built in the glass.
- **Method:**
  1. Fill the glass with cubed ice.
  2. Add prosecco, then Aperol, then soda.
  3. Stir gently once so you do not flatten the wine.
- **Specs:** 90 ml prosecco · 60 ml Aperol · 30 ml soda water
- **Garnish:** Orange slice.

## 8. Cosmopolitan

- **Glass:** Coupe.
- **Ice:** Cubed ice for shaking only. Serve straight up.
- **Method:**
  1. Shake vodka, triple sec, cranberry juice, and lime juice hard with ice.
  2. Fine-strain into the glass.
- **Specs:** 45 ml citrus vodka · 15 ml triple sec · 30 ml cranberry juice · 15 ml fresh lime juice
- **Garnish:** Orange peel or a lime wheel.

## 9. Daiquiri

- **Glass:** Coupe.
- **Ice:** Cubed ice for shaking only. Serve straight up.
- **Method:**
  1. Shake white rum, lime juice, and sugar syrup hard with ice.
  2. Fine-strain into the glass.
- **Specs:** 60 ml white rum · 25 ml fresh lime juice · 15 ml sugar syrup
- **Garnish:** Lime wheel (optional).

## 10. Whisky Sour

- **Glass:** Rocks, or a coupe if the guest wants it straight up.
- **Ice:** Cubed ice. Use fresh ice in the rocks glass.
- **Method:**
  1. Ask if the guest wants egg white. If yes, dry-shake all ingredients first (no ice), then shake hard with ice. If no, skip the dry shake.
  2. Strain over fresh ice, or fine-strain into a coupe if serving up.
  3. Bitters on the foam are optional.
- **Specs:** 50 ml bourbon or rye · 25 ml fresh lemon juice · 15 ml sugar syrup · 15 ml egg white (optional — ask the guest) · 2 dashes Angostura bitters, optional, on top
- **Garnish:** Lemon wheel and/or a cherry. Note the egg allergen on the ticket if used.

## 11. Piña Colada

- **Glass:** Hurricane, or a large highball if hurricane glasses are out.
- **Ice:** Cubed ice, blended (or shaken if the blender is down).
- **Method:**
  1. Blend rum, pineapple juice, coconut cream, lime juice, and a scoop of ice until smooth.
  2. If the blender is out of service, shake hard with ice and dump into the glass.
  3. Pour into the glass.
- **Specs:** 50 ml white rum · 75 ml pineapple juice · 50 ml coconut cream · 10 ml fresh lime juice
- **Garnish:** Pineapple wedge and a cherry. Serve with a straw.

## 12. If you are stuck

1. Re-read the spec. Measure again rather than guessing.
2. Ask the Bar Supervisor or Duty Manager (ext. 201).
3. Do not invent a house twist or substitute a different spirit unless a supervisor has approved it.
