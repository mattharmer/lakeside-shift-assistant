# New Starter Induction

**The Lakeside Hotel**  
Lake Macquarie, NSW  
Document 05 | Version 1.0 | November 2026  
Internal use only

## 1. Welcome

Welcome to The Lakeside Hotel, a licensed hotel in Lake Macquarie. We look after locals and visitors in the bar, dining room, beer garden, and gaming room.

This pack covers your first shift and first week. Read it before you start. Bring any questions to the Duty Manager (ext. 201). There are no silly questions on day one.

## 2. Before your first shift

Complete these with the Duty Manager or the person named on your roster email:

1. Paperwork: tax file number declaration, superannuation details, bank account, emergency contact, and right-to-work documents.
2. Certificates, as required for your role:
   - **RSA:** a current NSW Responsible Service of Alcohol competency if you will sell, serve, or supply liquor.
   - **RCG:** a current Responsible Conduct of Gambling competency if you will work in the gaming room.
   - **Food safety:** the venue's food-handler induction before you prepare or handle food. A Food Safety Supervisor is already appointed for the business; you still need the handler induction for kitchen or food-service work.
3. Bring your RSA and RCG competency (card or digital, as issued) on every shift that needs it. If it is expired or you cannot produce it, tell the Duty Manager before you start. You may not be able to work that role.
4. If you are unwell, especially with vomiting, diarrhoea, or a contagious illness, do not come in. Call the Duty Manager. For vomiting or diarrhoea, do not return to food handling until you have been symptom-free for at least 48 hours, or as the Duty Manager and food-safety rules require.

## 3. Your first shift

1. Aim to arrive 15 minutes before your rostered start time.
2. Park in the staff parking area, not in guest parks near the front door.
3. Enter through the staff door. If it is locked, wait and phone the Duty Manager on ext. 201 rather than following a patron in through the front.
4. Sign on as instructed, then find the Duty Manager and introduce yourself.
5. Leave bags, coats, and valuables in the staff room. The venue is not responsible for items left unsecured. Do not bring a large amount of cash onto the floor.
6. Put your phone in your bag or in the staff-room pigeonhole. Phones are not used on the floor except in an emergency or if the Duty Manager asks you to.
7. You will be buddied with an experienced staff member. Stay with them unless the Duty Manager moves you.
8. Walk the venue with your buddy before service: fire exits, first-aid kit, spill kit, amenities, office, and your work area.
9. At the end of the shift, sign off, check tomorrow's roster, and tell the Duty Manager how the shift went and anything you are unsure about.

## 4. Uniform and presentation

- Black venue shirt or polo (issued by the hotel), worn tucked in and clean.
- Black trousers or tailored black shorts as approved for your role. No ripped denim.
- Closed-in black shoes with non-slip soles. No sneakers with bright logos, and no open shoes in the kitchen or behind the bar.
- Name badge on the right chest, if one has been issued.
- Hair tied back if it can fall into food or drink. Keep nails short and clean. Minimal jewellery. No strong fragrance.
- A plain black jumper or the venue jacket may be worn in the beer garden in cool weather, if approved.
- If your uniform is damaged or you need a new size, ask the Duty Manager. Do not work a public-facing shift in a dirty or incomplete uniform.

Kitchen staff also wear the chef whites or kitchen uniform issued, plus a hat or hairnet as directed by the Head Chef.

## 5. RSA, RCG and food safety — what is expected

**RSA (all staff who serve liquor)**

- Follow Document 02 every shift.
- Do not serve liquor to a minor or to a person you believe is intoxicated.
- Check ID for anyone who looks under 25.
- If you need to refuse service or ask someone to leave, get the Duty Manager.

**RCG (gaming staff)**

- Do not work the gaming room unsupervised until your RCG is current and the Duty Manager has signed you off.
- Minors are not permitted in the gaming room.
- Never discuss a self-exclusion with other patrons.
- Get the Duty Manager for payout disputes, machine faults, or anyone who appears distressed about their gambling.

**Food safety (kitchen and food-service staff)**

- Wash hands before starting, after breaks, after handling rubbish or chemicals, and after touching your face or phone.
- Tell the Head Chef (ext. 202) about allergies on a ticket before the food is made.
- Do not work if you are ill, as in section 2.
- Record temperatures and follow the daily food-safety sheet. If a fridge or cool room is out of range, stop and tell the Head Chef and Duty Manager.

## 6. Where things are

Ask your buddy to show you each of these on day one:

- **Main entry and host stand:** guest arrival, bookings list, and menus.
- **Main bar and public bar:** POS terminals, EFTPOS, glasswasher, ice, spirit store access (Duty Manager or Bar Supervisor only), and the cocktail spec sheet (Document 03).
- **Dining room and beer garden:** table numbers, waiter's stations, and the path to the pass.
- **Kitchen and pass:** docket printer, allergen book, and staff meal process. Stay out of the cook's line unless you work there.
- **Gaming room:** entry, signage, and the attendant station. Do not enter to work unless you are rostered and signed off.
- **Amenities:** guest bathrooms and the staff toilet.
- **Office:** Duty Manager, incident registers, first-aid kit, lost property, and the safe. Knock before you enter. Do not open the safe.
- **Staff room:** sign-on, lockers or bag hooks, roster, and tea and coffee.
- **Cleaner's cupboard and spill kit:** mop, wet-floor signs, and vomit kit.
- **Fire exits and assembly point:** exits must stay clear. The assembly point is in the staff car park. If the alarm sounds, follow the Duty Manager or fire warden and do not use lifts if any are present.

Cool rooms, the bottle store, and the alarm panel are controlled by the Duty Manager or the Head Chef. Do not ask for codes.

## 7. Breaks, meals and finish times

1. Your unpaid or paid breaks will be explained on your first shift and follow the award and your roster. Do not leave the floor for a break until someone has taken over your section.
2. Take breaks in the staff room, not at a guest table and not at the bar in uniform with a drink.
3. Staff meals, if offered that shift, are ordered the way the Duty Manager or Head Chef shows you. Do not help yourself from the pass or the cool room.
4. You may drink tap water and tea or coffee in the staff room. Do not drink alcohol on shift, or in uniform after a shift on the premises, unless the Duty Manager has clearly approved a staff drink after close — and RSA still applies.
5. Finish times can move when trade is busy. If you must leave on time (transport, childcare, or another job), tell the Duty Manager at the start of the shift, not at the last minute.

## 8. Key policies

- **Be on time.** If you will be late or cannot work, phone the Duty Manager as soon as you know. Do not send a last-minute text and assume it has been seen.
- **Cash and comps.** Never leave a till unlocked or unattended. Do not give discounts or comps unless the Duty Manager has authorised them (see Document 06). Never take money, stock, or lost property home.
- **Respect.** Treat guests and colleagues courteously. Bullying, harassment, and discrimination are not accepted. Tell the Duty Manager if you feel unsafe or if you see this happen.
- **Social media and privacy.** Do not post photos of guests, staff, tills, or incidents. Do not discuss guests or incidents off the premises.
- **Smoking and vaping.** Only in the designated staff area, on a break, out of guest view, and away from the kitchen door.
- **Drugs.** Illegal drugs have no place at work. If you are impaired, you will be stood down and the Duty Manager will take it further.
- **Incidents.** Tell the Duty Manager about any refusal, injury, spill, complaint, or equipment fault. Do not wait until close.
- **Emergency.** For fire, medical emergency, or violence, call 000 if needed, then get the Duty Manager. Know your nearest exit.

The full staff policies are in the office. You will be asked to sign that you have read the key ones in your first week.

## 9. Who to ask

Start with the person closest to the task, then the Duty Manager if you are still unsure.

- **Duty Manager (ext. 201):** almost everything — roster, RSA, refusals, cash, complaints, incidents, uniform, and anything you cannot solve in a minute or two.
- **Bar Supervisor:** drink specs, pours, stock behind the bar, and how to use the cocktail sheet.
- **Head Chef or Kitchen Supervisor (ext. 202):** food tickets, allergens, staff meals, and kitchen areas.
- **Gaming Attendant (ext. 203):** gaming-room questions, only if you are signed off to work there.
- **Your buddy:** where things are, how the section runs, and "is this normal?" questions.

If two people give you different instructions, follow the Duty Manager and say so politely.

## 10. First-week checklist

Tick these with the Duty Manager by the end of your first week:

1. Paperwork and emergency contact are complete.
2. I have shown my RSA and/or RCG competency, if my role needs them.
3. I have completed the food-safety induction, if I handle food.
4. I have a fitting uniform, name badge, and a place to store my bag.
5. I know how to sign on and off, and where the roster is.
6. I have walked the venue: exits, assembly point, first-aid kit, spill kit, amenities, office, staff room, and my work area.
7. I have read Document 01 (open and close) for my area, even if I am not closing yet.
8. I have read Document 02 (RSA and incidents) and know to get the Duty Manager for refusals.
9. I have read Document 03 if I will make cocktails, and Document 04 if I will use POS or EFTPOS.
10. I have read Document 06 (complaints) and know I must not comp a bill without authority.
11. I have been shown how to use the POS and EFTPOS for my role, including a cancelled sale and a reprint.
12. I know when my breaks are and how to hand over my section.
13. I know who to call if I am sick or late.
14. I have asked the Duty Manager about anything I am still unsure of.

Keep this list. The Duty Manager will sign it off and file a copy in the office.
