# THE LAKESIDE HOTEL · Document 10
## Google Review Reply Guide

Version 1.0 | November 2026 · Internal use only

## 1. Purpose

Help the team draft short, consistent replies to public reviews. This builds on Document 06 (LAST: Listen, Apologise, Solve, Thank). Public replies are about tone, not settling the complaint online.

## 2. Tone of voice

- **Warm:** friendly, like a good local publican.
- **Local:** plain Australian English. Mention Lake Macquarie where it fits.
- **Brief:** 2 to 4 sentences.
- Say thanks, say sorry where it fits, and point to the next step.
- No jokes about the complaint. No sarcasm. No blame.

## 3. Rules (always)

1. ⚠️ **The manager approves every reply before it is posted.** Drafts are not posted by staff or by the assistant.
2. **No comps, refunds, discounts or vouchers offered in public.** Comps follow Document 06, section 3, and are handled in private.
3. **No staff names.** Sign off as "The Lakeside Hotel team" or [placeholder: manager role].
4. Never argue, correct the reviewer, or explain "our side" in public.
5. Never discuss a person's behaviour, intoxication, refusal, eviction, or any incident in public.
6. Do not admit legal liability or promise outcomes (Document 06, section 2.2).
7. Do not post guest details. Do not confirm whether someone was a guest.
8. Invite contact with the manager at [placeholder: venue email or phone]. Do not use a personal number.
9. Reply within [placeholder: target, for example 2 working days].

## 4. Templates

Replace the [placeholders]. Keep the reply short.

### 4.1 Five-star

> Thanks so much for the lovely review. We're really glad you enjoyed [placeholder: food, drinks, view, service]. We hope to see you back at The Lakeside Hotel soon.
> The Lakeside Hotel team

### 4.2 Four-star

> Thanks for the great feedback. We're happy you enjoyed [placeholder: what they liked], and we appreciate you telling us what could be better. We'll share it with the team. Hope to see you again soon.
> The Lakeside Hotel team

### 4.3 Mixed (some good, some not)

> Thanks for taking the time to tell us about your visit. We're glad [placeholder: the good part], and we're sorry [placeholder: the part that fell short] wasn't up to the standard we want. We're passing your feedback to the team. If you'd like to talk it through, please contact the manager at [placeholder: contact].
> The Lakeside Hotel team

### 4.4 One or two stars

⚠️ **Flag for the manager before drafting is finalised.**

> Thanks for letting us know, and we're sorry your visit wasn't what it should have been. We'd like to understand what happened. Please contact the manager at [placeholder: contact] so we can look into it properly.
> The Lakeside Hotel team

Do not offer a refund, comp or voucher. Do not repeat the specific complaint in detail.

### 4.5 Review about an RSA refusal or eviction

⚠️ **Always flag for the manager. Do not post until the Duty Manager and manager have checked the incident register (Document 02).**

> Thanks for your feedback. We take every review seriously, and we can't discuss individual matters in a public reply. If you'd like to speak with us, please contact the manager at [placeholder: contact].
> The Lakeside Hotel team

Do not say whether the person was refused or evicted. Do not mention alcohol, behaviour or an incident. Do not argue. Do not discuss the person or the night.

## 5. Drafting steps

1. Read the full review. Note the star rating and the main points.
2. Check whether it mentions RSA, refusal, eviction, a minor, injury, illness, discrimination, or legal action. If yes, use 4.5 or 4.4 and flag it.
3. Pick the matching template. Adjust two or three words to fit.
4. Check the rules in section 3.
5. Pass the draft to the manager. The manager edits and approves.
6. Log any real complaint in the complaints log (Document 06, section 5).
