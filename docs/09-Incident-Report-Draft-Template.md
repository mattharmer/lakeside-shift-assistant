## 1. Purpose and rules

This template helps staff draft an incident entry in line with Document 02, section 7. It does not replace the liquor incident register or the gambling incident register. The Duty Manager decides which register the final entry goes in.

1. 🚨 If anyone is hurt or in danger, call **000** first, then get the Duty Manager (ext. 201). Draft the report later.
2. Write only what was seen or said. Where a fact is not known, write **not provided**. Do not guess.
3. Describe people by what you saw (approximate age, clothing, height, distinguishing features). Do not guess names, ages or reasons.
4. Do not use unexplained acronyms.
5. Record as soon as practicable, and within 24 hours.
6. Do not discuss the incident on social media or with other patrons.
