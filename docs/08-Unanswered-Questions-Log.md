# THE LAKESIDE HOTEL · Document 08
## Unanswered Questions Log

Version 1.0 | November 2026 · Internal use only

## 1. Purpose

When the Venue Ops Assistant cannot answer from Documents 01 to 07, it sends the staff member to the Duty Manager (ext. 201). This log records those questions. Patterns show the owner which procedure to write or fix next.

## 2. Who keeps it

- **Duty Manager:** adds entries during or after the shift.
- **Owner:** [placeholder: Licensee, Approved Manager or Operations Manager] reviews the log and assigns fixes.

## 3. How to add an entry

1. Copy the assistant's GAP LOG line, or write the question in the staff member's own words.
2. Fill in one row in the table below.
3. Pick the closest area: bar, gaming, kitchen, tech, RSA, or other.
4. Suggest which document to write or update.
5. Do not write passwords, codes, or guest details in the log.
6. ⚠️ If the question was about safety, RSA, a minor, or an incident, tell the owner the same day. Do not wait for the weekly review.

## 5. Weekly 10-minute review

The Duty Manager and owner do this once a week. Suggested time: [placeholder].

1. **Minutes 0–2:** Count open entries. Note any asked more than once.
2. **Minutes 2–5:** Sort by area. Pick the top 3 repeat questions.
3. **Minutes 5–8:** For each, decide: add to an existing document, write a new one, or confirm "ask the Duty Manager" is the right answer.
4. **Minutes 8–10:** Assign an owner and a due date. Update status. Mark fixed items with the document and version.
5. ⚠️ Anything touching RSA, gaming, safety, or licence conditions is checked against the venue licence and current Liquor & Gaming NSW guidance by the owner before the document is changed.
6. When a document changes, update the version number and tell the team at the next briefing.
