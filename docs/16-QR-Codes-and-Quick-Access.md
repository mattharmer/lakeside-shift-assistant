# THE LAKESIDE HOTEL · Document 16
## QR Codes and Quick Access

Version 1.0 | November 2026 · Internal use only

## 1. Purpose

Put the right procedure one scan away, at the place it is needed. Staff can also ask the assistant a question by voice, hands-free.

## 2. Rules

1. QR codes open **internal staff pages only**. Do not put them where guests can reach them.
2. Never put passwords, safe codes or alarm codes on a QR page or sign.
3. Each QR code links to [placeholder: staff assistant or document link]. The manager creates and maintains the links.
4. Check every code works once a month. Replace damaged or faded labels.
5. If a QR code does not work, ask the Duty Manager (ext. 201). Paper copies of Documents 01 to 06 are kept in the office.
6. 🚨 In an emergency, call **000** first. Do not scan, search or type first.

## 4. Label wording

Put a short label under each code, in large text:

- "Scan for: [what it opens]"
- "Not sure? Ask the Duty Manager (ext. 201)"

For the bar and gaming room, add: "RSA and incidents: get the Duty Manager first."

## 5. Asking the assistant by voice (hands-free)

Use your phone's built-in voice typing. It is a typing tool, so nothing special is needed.

1. Open the staff assistant [placeholder: link or app].
2. Tap the text box so the keyboard appears.
3. Tap the **microphone** icon on the keyboard.
4. Speak slowly and clearly. Keep it to one question.
5. Check the words on screen. Fix anything that is wrong.
6. Send it.
7. Read the short steps. If you are mid-service, say "next step" or ask the Duty Manager.

Tips:

- Say the document or task, for example "Closing the bar" or "Margarita spec".
- Do not say passwords, safe codes or alarm codes out loud or type them.
- Do not say guest names or personal details.
- Voice typing may mishear. For RSA, refusals, injuries or incidents, always get the Duty Manager as well.
- Phones are not used on the floor except in an emergency or if the Duty Manager asks (Document 05, section 3). Use the assistant in the staff room, the office, or when the Duty Manager has OK'd it.
