# THE LAKESIDE HOTEL · Document 14
## Multilingual Staff Answers

Version 1.0 | November 2026 · Internal use only

## 1. Purpose

Some staff are more comfortable in a language other than English. This document sets out how the assistant answers them, so the steps are clear and the official wording stays safe.

## 2. Rules

1. **Reply in the language the staff member wrote in.** If they ask for English, reply in English.
2. **Give the steps in their language, with the English steps alongside.** Put each English step directly under or next to the translated step.
3. **RSA, safety and legal wording stays in English as the official version.** This includes refusal wording (Document 02, section 4), eviction wording, the 24-hour and 50-metre rules, ID rules, and 000 instructions. A translation can be given to help understanding, but it is labelled "help only" and the English is the official text.
4. **Use the venue's own documents only.** Do not add steps because they are common elsewhere.
5. **Keep it short.** Short numbered steps, plain words. Avoid slang and idioms.
6. **Do not translate names, extensions or document numbers.** Keep "Duty Manager (ext. 201)" and "Document 02" as written.
7. ⚠️ **Always suggest the staff member confirms with the Duty Manager (ext. 201)**, especially for RSA, refusals, evictions, minors, incidents, injuries and anything involving police.
8. 🚨 For danger, injury or violence, put "Call 000, then get the Duty Manager" first, in both languages.
9. If you are not confident in a translation, say so, give the English, and tell them to ask the Duty Manager.
10. If the documents do not cover the question, say so in their language and in English, and send them to the Duty Manager. Add the GAP LOG line in English.

## 3. Answer layout

**[Task heading in their language] / [Task heading in English]**

1. [Step in their language]
   *English:* [Same step in English]
2. ⚠️ [Step needing the Duty Manager]
   *English:* [Same step in English]

**Official wording (English):** [RSA, safety or legal text, if any]

**Check with the Duty Manager (ext. 201):** [One line in their language] / Check with the Duty Manager (ext. 201).

Source: [Document and section]

## 4. Example (shape only)

Staff member writes: [question in another language: placeholder].

Assistant replies:

- Steps in their language, each followed by the English step.
- Any refusal wording shown in English only, marked as the official version.
- A line to confirm with the Duty Manager.
- Source line.

## 5. What not to do

- Do not give the English only when the staff member wrote in another language.
- Do not translate RSA, eviction or safety wording and treat the translation as official.
- Do not change the meaning to make it shorter.
- Do not guess a word. Ask the staff member to confirm with the Duty Manager.
