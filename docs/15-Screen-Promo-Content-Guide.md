# THE LAKESIDE HOTEL · Document 15
## Screen Promo Content Guide

Version 1.0 | November 2026 · Internal use only

## 1. Purpose

Make short promo clips for the venue's TV screens using the specs in Document 03 and the current specials. Clips must follow responsible service (Document 02).

## 2. Rules

1. ⚠️ **The manager approves every clip before it goes on a screen.**
2. Use only drinks and ingredients from Document 03, or specials the manager has given you. Do not invent drinks or ingredients.
3. **Price is [placeholder].** Do not make up prices. The manager supplies them.
4. Do not show or say quantities, or measures that suggest speed or volume.
5. Do not show anyone who looks under 18. Do not use cartoons, toys or themes that appeal to minors.
6. Do not show real people, branded characters or music you do not have the right to use.
7. If a screen is used for gaming-room or statutory signage, do not replace it. Tell the Duty Manager (Document 04, section 8).

## 3. The 15-second script format

| Seconds | Part | What to write |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | **Hook** | One short line that gets attention. No pressure to drink. |
| 3–7 | **Drink name** | The drink name and glass. |
| 7–12 | **3 key ingredients** | Three ingredients from the Document 03 spec. No volumes needed. |
| 12–15 | **Call to action** | Price [placeholder] and a relaxed invitation (for example, "Ask at the bar"). |

Write the script in plain Australian English. Keep each part to one short sentence.

## 4. On-screen caption rules

1. **Large text** that can be read from across the room.
2. **6 words or fewer per frame.**
3. **House colours:** [placeholder: colours or hex codes, font].
4. High contrast. Keep text clear of the screen edges.
5. One idea per frame.
6. Include the price [placeholder] only on the last frame.
7. Use the venue name or logo [placeholder] on the last frame.

## 5. Responsible-service wording

Never use:

- "Drink fast", "down it", "skol", "shots", "bottomless", "all you can drink", "get smashed", "get wasted".
- Quantity or volume promotions (for example "buy more, pay less", jugs or rounds as a rush).
- Anything that links drinking to winning, success, or getting around the rules.
- Anything aimed at minors.

Use instead:

- "Enjoy responsibly", "take your time", "pair with a meal", "ask about our food and non-alcoholic options".
- Include a responsible-service line on at least one frame: [placeholder: approved wording from the manager or licence].

## 6. Rotation plan

Run a mix of food, drinks and non-alcoholic options. The manager sets the mix.

| Session | Time | Content focus | Clips |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lunch | [placeholder] | Food specials, coffee, non-alcoholic drinks, one light cocktail such as the Aperol Spritz | [placeholder] |
| Afternoon | [placeholder] | Beer garden drinks, the Mojito, the Piña Colada, snacks | [placeholder] |
| Evening | [placeholder] | Classic cocktails such as the Margarita, the Negroni, the Old Fashioned, the Espresso Martini | [placeholder] |

Rotate clips so no one drink repeats back to back. Refresh the playlist every [placeholder: period].

## 7. Making a clip

1. Pick the drink from Document 03 or a current special.
2. Write the 15-second script using section 3.
3. Write the captions using section 4.
4. Check section 5 wording.
5. Write an **image or video prompt** for the AI tool: describe the glass, ice, garnish and setting from the Document 03 spec, in house colours, with no people under 18, no logos you do not own, and no text in the image (captions are added separately).
6. Give the script, captions and prompt to the manager.
7. ⚠️ **Manager to approve before it goes on a screen.**
8. Record the clip name and approval date in [placeholder: log].

## 8. Example prompt shape

> A [glass type from spec] of [drink name] with [ice from spec] and [garnish from spec], on a [placeholder: bar top or beer garden table] in warm evening light at a lakeside hotel. House colours [placeholder]. No people. No text. 15 seconds, slow camera move.
