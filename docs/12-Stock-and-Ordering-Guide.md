# THE LAKESIDE HOTEL · Document 12
## Stock and Ordering Guide

Version 1.0 | November 2026 · Internal use only

## 1. Purpose

Show how to count stock at the end of the night, compare it to par levels, and draft an order list. **The manager always checks and sends the order.** Staff and the assistant never send orders.

## 2. Rules

1. Use only counts that staff actually took. Write **not counted** for anything missed. Do not estimate.
2. Do not invent products, quantities, prices or suppliers. The rows below are generic examples until the manager sets the venue's own list.
3. Spirit store and cool room access follow Document 05, section 6 and Document 01. Do not ask for codes.
4. Count with a second person where possible.
5. Report any shortfall, breakage or suspected loss to the Duty Manager (ext. 201). Do not adjust counts to match.

## 3. End-of-night count

Do this after last drinks and tidy-up, before lock-up (Document 01, sections 11 and 14).

1. Get the stock sheet and a pen. ☐
2. Start at one end of the area and work in one direction. ☐
3. Count full units (bottles, kegs, cases, boxes) and write partial units as a fraction or a measure (for example, "half a bottle"). ☐
4. Note damaged, out-of-date or expired stock separately. Do not count it as usable. ☐
5. Record the count in the "On hand" column. ☐
6. Check fridges and restock for the morning where possible (Document 01, section 11, step 4). ☐
7. Write down items that ran out during the night, and 86'd items. ☐
8. Hand the sheet to the Duty Manager. ☐
9. ⚠️ Kitchen stock is counted by the Head Chef or Kitchen Supervisor (ext. 202), who also follows the food-safety sheet and records any discards.

## 6. Drafting an order list

1. Take the completed count sheet.
2. For each product below par, work out short by = par minus on hand.
3. Group items by supplier area, as set out in the par table.
4. List product, unit, and quantity to order. Round up to full cases or kegs only if the manager's par table says so.
5. Add any run-outs and 86'd items the team reported.
6. Flag anything not counted, damaged, or unclear.
7. Do not add prices. Prices come from the supplier price list the manager holds.
8. Hand the draft to the manager.
9. ⚠️ **The manager checks the draft, changes it if needed, and sends the order.** Do not send it to a supplier yourself.
